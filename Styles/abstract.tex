\begin{abstract}
In this paper, we propose \textbf{RLearner-LLM}, a novel alignment framework that combines Natural Language Inference (NLI) entailment probabilities with a Multiplicative ACR Gate reward to create a holistic preference signal leveraging Hybrid Direct Preference Optimization (Hybrid-DPO). We demonstrate that RLearner-LLM mitigates the ``alignment tax,'' improving NLI entailment scores by up to 7.6$\times$ over Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) baselines on LLaMA-2-13B across five academic domains spanning Biology, Medicine, and Law, while delivering consistent Answer Coverage Rate (ACR) improvements across all three evaluated architectures (LLaMA-2-13B, LLaMA-3-8B, and Qwen3-8B). We show that purely additive reward signals suffer from DPO alignment tax, whereas our multiplicative gate—which conditions the reward on hard answer-coverage constraints—reliably preserves logical grounding. Our qualitative analysis across all five domains confirms that Hybrid-DPO uniquely anchors model explanations in rigorous logic without sacrificing pedagogical fluency.
\end{abstract}
