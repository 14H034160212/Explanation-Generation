\section{Conclusion}

\textbf{RLearner-LLM} closes DPO's reward-signal blindspot with a dual-signal Hybrid reward (NLI $+$ verifier; $H_A$/$H_M$ by pair-count feasibility). Across $5$ domains and $3$ architectures it improves NLI in $11/15$ cells (up to $6.6\times$ over SFT) and is the first single-pass-RL method to surpass ILearner-LLM (K=5) on Auckland Law; its symmetric $95\%$ win over its own SFT and $95\%$ loss to GPT-4o-mini's longer outputs under one judge replicate verbosity bias on a frontier comparator, motivating logic-aware metrics over LLM-as-a-judge in knowledge-intensive generation. Going beyond entailment, a pairwise reasoning-quality reward with a length-normalised objective (SimPO) yields explanations that independent judges and human raters prefer to SFT \emph{at matched length}, a first length-controlled step toward reasoning-faithful tuning.
