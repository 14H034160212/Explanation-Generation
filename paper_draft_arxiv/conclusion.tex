\section{Conclusion}

We trained educational explanation generators with DPO on preference pairs labelled by an NLI entailment score, and checked the gains with a held-out scorer and an answer-masking test. On LLaMA-2-13B the large entailment gains were answer restatement; on Gemma~4 E4B they survived both checks with shorter outputs. Adding a verifier score to the NLI signal changed little, and on a shared candidate pool it only diluted the NLI gain. A pairwise reasoning-judge reward trained with SimPO produced explanations that LLM judges and human raters prefer at matched length, and on five courses the NLI-trained policy, with the largest entailment gain, was judged below SFT. Summing the judge and NLI scores on length-matched pairs kept a significant entailment gain without that cost. Entailment rewards are cheap and can help, but they should always be reported with the masking test and output length, and they do not replace a measure of reasoning.
