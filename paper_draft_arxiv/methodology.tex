\section{Method}
\label{sec:methodology}

\subsection{Scores}
\label{sec:first_principles}
For a question with correct option text $A$ and a candidate explanation $E$ we compute:
\begin{itemize}
\item $S_\text{NLI}(E)$, the probability that $E$ entails $A$ (premise $E$, hypothesis $A$) under \texttt{cross-encoder/nli-deberta-v3-small}, with joint length 512 and no prompt wrapping.
\item $S_\text{ver}(E)\in\{1,\dots,5\}$, the Likert rating of the Alpaca-7B explanation verifier of \citet{bao2025exploring}, and its rescaling $\tilde S_\text{ver}=(S_\text{ver}-1)/4\in[0,1]$.
\item $\mathrm{ACR}(E)$, the answer-coverage rate: the fraction of the option's words of $\geq$4 letters that appear in $E$.
\item $S_\text{NLI}^\text{mask}(E)$, the \emph{answer-masked} NLI score: $S_\text{NLI}$ computed after replacing, in $E$, the option text and every word that starts with one of its key terms by \texttt{[MASK]} (\S\ref{sec:masking}).
\end{itemize}

\centering

\midrule

Masked NLI & $S_\text{NLI}^\text{mask}\cdot \mathbf{1}[\mathrm{ACR}\geq 0.5]$ \\

\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

The multiplicative form $H_M$ was used for Gemma~4 and for Qwen3 on the two biology datasets, $H_A$ elsewhere (Appendix~\ref{sec:variant_assignment_appendix}); the choice followed our experimental order and has no principled basis, and the two give similar results (Appendix~\ref{sec:variant_ablation_appendix}). The masked-NLI variant is new in this version: it asks the explanation to mention the answer (the ACR gate) but rewards only the entailment that remains once the answer's own words are hidden, so restating the answer earns nothing.

\paragraph{What a pair does and does not guarantee.} A pair is ordered by a single scalar score, so the chosen explanation need not be better on every component. In our pools the chosen explanation has a lower verifier score than the rejected one in 4--34\% of pairs and a lower NLI score in 1--8\%; the remainder are mostly verifier ties, because the 1--5 verifier often rates all three candidates alike (Appendix~\ref{sec:pair_stats}). In the Gemma~4 pool the mean verifier score of chosen and rejected explanations is 3.05 and 3.06, i.e.\ the pairs are ranked by NLI. We also test a variant that keeps only pairs in which the chosen explanation is at least as good on both NLI and verifier (\S\ref{sec:single_dual}).

\subsection{Training}
\label{sec:hybrid_dpo}
Pairs $(x,y^+,y^-)$ are used with the standard DPO loss \citep{rafailov2023direct},
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_\text{DPO} = -\mathbb{E}\Bigl[\log\sigma\Bigl(\beta\log\tfrac{\pi_\theta(y^+|x)}{\pi_\text{ref}(y^+|x)} - \beta\log\tfrac{\pi_\theta(y^-|x)}{\pi_\text{ref}(y^-|x)}\Bigr)\Bigr],
\label{eq:dpo_loss}
\end{equation}
with the SFT model as $\pi_\text{ref}$. For the reasoning-judge reward we also use SimPO \citep{meng2024simpo}, which length-normalises the log-likelihood and needs no reference model (\S\ref{sec:reasoning-reward}).

\paragraph{Implementation notes.} Qwen3-8B applies RMSNorm to $q,k$ after the projection reshape, so its LoRA targets exclude \texttt{q\_proj}/\texttt{k\_proj}. Gemma~4 E4B-it is a multimodal checkpoint whose text-tower weights must be loaded through \texttt{Gemma4ForConditionalGeneration} with a full-path LoRA regex (Appendix~\ref{sec:impl_notes_appendix}). At inference the DPO adapter is applied on top of the SFT-merged base, since that is the reference it was trained against.
