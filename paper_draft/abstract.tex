\begin{abstract}
In this paper, we propose \textbf{RLearner-LLM}, a novel alignment framework that combines Natural Language Inference (NLI) entailment probabilities with fluency verifier scores to create a holistic reward signal leveraging Hybrid Direct Preference Optimization (Hybrid-DPO). We demonstrate that RLearner-LLM eliminates the "alignment tax," improving NLI entailment scores by up to 5x over Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) baselines on medical and biological datasets, while maintaining high answer coverage and linguistic naturalness. Our 8B parameter model achieves a 95\% win rate against SFT in GPT-4o-mini pairwise evaluations.
\end{abstract}
