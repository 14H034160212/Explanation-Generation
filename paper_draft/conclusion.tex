\section{Conclusion}
This paper introduces \textbf{RLearner-LLM}, a cross-domain methodology for mitigating hallucination and stylistic verbosity bias in context-driven Natural Language Generation. By fusing NLI entailment mechanics with verifier fluency scores via Hybrid-DPO, RLearner-LLM algorithmically constructs preference datasets that shatter the alignment tax, demonstrating that logical consistency can be achieved without sacrificing conversational quality. Our locally-hosted 8B and 13B student models eclipse proprietary commercial baselines (GPT-3.5 and 4o-mini), particularly in specialized domains like Cardiff Biology (NLI 0.32) and Sydney Biology (NLI 0.35). The success of this approach on unseen law and medicine datasets paves the way for trustworthy, open-source educational deployment where factual consistency is non-negotiable. Future work will explore applying this framework to broader reasoning tasks and varying hyperparameters dynamically across specialized knowledge domains.
