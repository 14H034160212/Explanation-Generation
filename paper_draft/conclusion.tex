\section{Conclusion}

We presented \textbf{RLearner-LLM}, a cross-domain alignment framework that targets the root cause of logical misalignment in DPO: a reward-signal blindspot that reinforces verbosity over logical correctness. The Hybrid reward fuses NLI entailment with a verifier fluency score under an ACR gate, producing preference pairs whose chosen members are simultaneously logically grounded and linguistically natural---without human annotation. Across five academic domains and three base architectures (LLaMA-2-13B, Qwen3-8B, Gemma 4 E4B-it), Hybrid-DPO improves NLI in $12$ of $15$ cells (up to $6\times$ over SFT), achieves the first single-pass-RL result to surpass the iterative ILearner-LLM (K=5) benchmark on Auckland Law, replicates the verifier-blindness finding on a fourth corpus via a Tier-B robustness ablation, and wins $95\%$ of blind pairwise comparisons against GPT-4o-mini. The framework generalises to any task with a verifiable logical structure (medical reasoning, mathematical proof, code generation); natural extensions include dynamic weighting of the hybrid components and online-RLHF deployment.
