Which explanation reasons better?

A blinded study of AI-written explanations for multiple-choice exam questions from university biology, law and medicine courses. 10 questions, about 10 minutes. 本研究为盲评，共 10 题，大约 10 分钟。

How to judge

  1. Each question shows the correct answer. Two explanations of why it is correct follow, labelled A and B. They come from two different systems, in random order.
  2. Pick the explanation whose reasoning better justifies the correct answer: the facts are right and the steps actually lead to the answer.
  3. Ignore length, formatting and style. An explanation that only restates the answer without a reason is worse.
  4. Choose Tie if they are equally good or equally bad. Choose Outside my expertise if you cannot judge the subject.
